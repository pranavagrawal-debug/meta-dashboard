How to read these charts. Good lead % trend shows whether the leads coming in are getting better or worse (use it to decide which campaigns or ads to scale or pause). L2O % trend shows how many leads actually turn into orders. Each line is a trailing window: the point on 15 Nov for T-7 covers leads from 9 to 15 Nov. ■ T-7 reacts fast but is noisy, ■ T-14 is the middle view, ■ T-30 is the smoothest and most reliable. Click a legend item to hide or show that line. Gaps mean no leads in that window. The last few days are always understated because those leads haven't matured yet.
Good lead = Interested or Converted, frozen: once a lead has been seen as Interested or Converted in any upload it stays good, even if the CRM later moves it to Lost or Follow-up. L2O = Converted ÷ Leads. Click any row to see its good leads and to scope the charts to it. Charts show, for each day, the trailing T-7 / T-14 / T-30 window ending that day, grouped by lead-form date; recent points are immature. Green/red on Good % = top/bottom third among rows meeting min leads. * = below min leads. No spend data, so no cost metrics. Uploads are read in your browser and shared with the whole team. Shared storage holds lead IDs, statuses, ad copy and tags only. Names and phone numbers are never stored, so they appear only in the session that uploaded the files.
