How ROAS is calculated. ROAS = revenue ÷ ad spend. Spend comes from the Ads Manager export you upload (export with Breakdown: Day, at ad level, with Campaign name, Ad set name, Ad name, Amount spent, Impressions and Link clicks). Revenue is what you upload (a file with phone number and amount) or type against each lead: click any Leads, Converted or Revenue number to open the lead list, then type the amount in the Revenue (₹) box next to each lead (it works for any lead). For the window you pick, spend is the Meta spend on those days and revenue is from leads created in the same days, so recent windows are understated until leads convert. CTR uses link clicks if the export has them, otherwise all clicks. * = fewer leads than Min leads. Green = ROAS of 1 or more, red = below 1. Uploading a new export updates the days it contains and keeps older days. A revenue file is matched to leads by the last 10 digits of the phone number; if a phone has several leads, the revenue goes to the most recent one, and several rows for one phone are added together. Uploading again replaces the revenue on the leads it matches.
How to read these charts. Good lead % trend shows whether the leads coming in are getting better or worse (use it to decide which campaigns or ads to scale or pause). L2O % trend shows how many leads actually turn into orders. Each line is a trailing window: the point on 15 Nov for T-7 covers leads from 9 to 15 Nov. ■ T-7 reacts fast but is noisy, ■ T-14 is the middle view, ■ T-30 is the smoothest and most reliable. Click a legend item to hide or show that line. Gaps mean no leads in that window. The last few days are always understated because those leads haven't matured yet.
Good lead = Interested or Converted, frozen: once a lead has been seen as Interested or Converted in any upload it stays good, even if the CRM later moves it to Lost or Follow-up. L2O = Converted ÷ Leads (includes OPD booked). Actual L2O = Converted with sub-status OPD VISITED ÷ Leads. Click any row to see its good leads and to scope the charts to it. Charts show, for each day, the trailing T-7 / T-14 / T-30 window ending that day, grouped by CRM created date; recent points are immature. Green/red on Good % = top/bottom third among rows meeting min leads. * = below min leads. No spend data, so no cost metrics. Uploads are read in your browser and shared with the whole team. Shared storage holds lead IDs, names, phone numbers, statuses, ad copy and tags, and is protected by the team password.
